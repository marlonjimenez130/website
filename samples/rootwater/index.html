Most plumbers fix pipes. We protect Naples homes from water: the slow leak under the slab, the heater squeezed into a condo closet, the hard water etching your shower glass, and the drip nobody hears until the floor buckles.

How a visit works

No surprises, start to finish.

  1. 01

    We find it.

    Listening gear and a thermal camera find hidden leaks, even under a slab, before anyone breaks tile.

  2. 02

    You see it, and the price.

    We text you a photo of the problem and a written price. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  3. 03

    We fix it right.

    The joint is cut, sealed and tightened properly: fixed on the first visit whenever the part is on the van.

  4. 04

    Dry, tested, done.

    We run the water, check every joint we touched, and leave the floor drier than we found it.

What we fix

The six calls Naples homes make most.

Each one with the signs to look for, so you know when it's time to call.

All services and prices →

  • Leaks and slab leaks

    Most homes here sit on a concrete slab, so a hidden leak can run under the floor for months. We find it with listening and thermal tools before anyone breaks tile.

    SignsA warm spot on the floor, a water bill that jumps, running water you can hear with every tap off.

    See leak detection →

  • Water heaters

    Tank and tankless, repaired or replaced, including the tight ones in garages and condo closets.

    SignsLukewarm showers, rusty hot water, popping sounds, a puddle by the tank.

    See water heaters →

  • Drains and sewer lines

    Kitchen, shower and main lines cleared, with a camera check so you can see what caused the clog.

    SignsGurgling, slow drains in more than one room, a sewer smell in the yard.

    See drains and sewer →

  • Hard water and softeners

    Naples water runs moderately hard, and harder on a private well. We test it first, then suggest a softener or filter only if it helps.

    SignsWhite crust on faucets, spotty glasses, a shower door that never looks clean.

    See hard water →

  • Repiping

    Many homes built from the late 1970s to the mid 1990s have gray polybutylene pipe, which can fail without warning. We replace it with PEX or copper, one area at a time.

    SignsGray plastic pipe at the heater or under sinks, pinhole leaks that keep coming back.

    See repiping →

  • Seasonal home checks

    Heading north for the summer? We shut the water off the right way, check the heater and every tap, then do it again before you fly back.

    SignsYou'll be away more than a month, or a neighbor says your irrigation is running.

    See seasonal checks →

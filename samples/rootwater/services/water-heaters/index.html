Signs you need it

When to call.

  • Showers go lukewarm sooner than they used to.
  • Rusty or cloudy hot water.
  • Popping or rumbling from the tank.
  • Water or rust at the base of the tank.
  • The breaker trips or the pilot keeps going out.
  • You can't remember when it was put in.

What's going on in there

Scale settles. Tanks tire.

Minerals in the water settle at the bottom of a tank as scale. It makes the heater work harder and can make it pop and rumble. Not sure how old yours is? The date is usually on the label, often hidden in the serial number. Text us a photo of the label and we'll read it for you.

Heaters in tight places.

Many Naples condos and villas keep the water heater in a closet, a utility room or a corner of the garage. In a condo, a leaking tank can become your neighbor's ceiling. Tank, tankless or heat pump.

  • Tank

    The simplest swap, and the lowest price up front.

  • Tankless

    Hot water on demand and a smaller footprint. ENERGY STAR gives tankless heaters a life expectancy of about 20 years, longer than tank models.

  • Heat pump

    Uses electricity to move heat instead of making it. It needs a warm space with room around it, which many Naples garages have.

Source for the tankless life expectancy: ENERGY STAR.

Water heater questions

Repair or replace?

If the tank itself leaks, replace it. If it's a part, repair it, unless the heater is old enough that you'd be paying for parts on borrowed time. We show you both prices.

Is tankless worth it?

It lasts longer and saves space, and it costs more to fit, especially when we're changing gas lines or electrical service. If your household is small and the tank is healthy, it may not pay back. We'll tell you.

Should I drain my tank?

Draining a little water from the bottom once a year helps clear scale on many tanks. We'll show you how, or do it on a seasonal check.

Signs you need it

When to call.

  • More than one drain is slow.
  • The toilet gurgles when the washer drains.
  • Water comes up in the shower, the lowest drain in the house.
  • A sewer smell indoors or in the yard.
  • A soggy, extra-green patch of lawn over the line.
  • The same clog keeps coming back.

See it for yourself

We show you the cause.

A clog is a symptom. Grease, tree roots finding their way into a joint, a sagging section of pipe: the camera shows which one, so you fix the cause once instead of the clog every year.

Drain and sewer questions

Should I use a chemical drain cleaner first?

We'd rather you didn't. They often don't reach a deep clog, and they can be hard on older pipes and on whoever opens the drain next. Call us instead.

Who's responsible for the sewer line?

Usually the line on your property is yours and the main in the street is the utility's. Your utility can confirm where the line changes hands.

Will it come back?

If it's roots or a sagging pipe, it may. That's why we put a camera down it: you see the cause and decide whether to fix it now or keep an eye on it.

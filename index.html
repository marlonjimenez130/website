Accessibility

I aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The page works with a keyboard, follows your setting for reduced motion, reads in full without JavaScript, and every film that plays by itself for more than five seconds has a Pause button. I tested it on 12 November 2026 in Google Chrome, with automated checks (contrast, keyboard, links, layout) and by keyboard; it hasn't yet been checked by a person using a screen reader. If anything here doesn't work for you, email me and I'll fix it or send you the same information another way.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking or analytics. The fonts (Newsreader and Cabin, both open-source) are stored on this site, so your browser doesn't ask Google or anyone else for them. The email links open your own email app; nothing is sent from this page. The service that hosts these pages may keep standard access logs.
